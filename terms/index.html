What DriverTakings is

DriverTakings is a tool to help self-employed drivers record their takings and working costs and understand what they've actually earned. On paid plans, DriverTakings can help structure your records for HMRC's Making Tax Digital for Income Tax Self Assessment (MTD ITSA) service, and, where you choose to connect your HMRC account, submit summary figures to HMRC on your behalf when you explicitly confirm a submission.

This is not tax, legal or financial advice

DriverTakings provides estimates and organisational tools based on figures you enter. It does not replace an accountant, and figures such as estimated tax and National Insurance provisions are simplified estimates, not a tax calculation. You are responsible for the accuracy of the figures you enter and for reviewing anything before it is submitted to HMRC. We strongly recommend speaking to a qualified accountant about your specific tax position, particularly around how your expenses should be categorised.

Your responsibilities

You must provide accurate information, keep your login credentials secure, and review any information carefully before confirming an HMRC submission — DriverTakings will show you exactly what will be sent and will never submit anything without your explicit confirmation. You are responsible for meeting your own HMRC filing obligations and deadlines; DriverTakings is a tool to help, not a substitute for that responsibility.
